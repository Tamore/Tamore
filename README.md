### 🔬 Research & Systems Focus

I am a technical engineer specializing in **distributed systems**, **event-driven architectures**, **microservice coordination**, and **system observability**.

- ⚙️ **Event-Driven Architectures:** Engineering multi-container coordination clusters, asynchronous stream processing via Redis Streams, and W3C trace-context propagation using OpenTelemetry and Jaeger.
- ☁️ **Cloud Infrastructure:** Architecting scalable backend services with Django, FastAPI, Docker, and Supabase.
- 🤖 **Autonomous AI Systems:** Researching event-driven multi-agent coordination frameworks and asynchronous AI task pipelines.
